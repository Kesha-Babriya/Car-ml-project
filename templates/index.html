Used-Car Inspection Checklist

Review essential mechanical, documentation, and test-drive checkpoints before buying to identify hidden risks and negotiate with confidence.

Checklist Progress: 0 of 15 completed
CATEGORY 01

🔧 Mechanical & Exterior Checks

Inspect under the bonnet, exterior bodywork, and chassis condition.

Engine Condition & Fluid Leaks

⚠️ Critical Check

Check for abnormal exhaust smoke (blue, black, or thick white), engine knocking, oil sludge beneath oil filler cap, or fresh fluid leaks.

Braking System & Fluid

Check brake pad thickness, ensure rotor discs are smooth without deep ridges, and confirm brake fluid reservoir is between Min and Max.

Tyres & Tread Depth

Ensure tyres have even tread (at least 2.5–3 mm remaining), no sidewall cracks or bulges, and verify a usable spare tyre and jack are present.

Lights & In-Cabin Electronics

Test headlights (high/low beam), indicators, brake lights, air conditioning cooling, heater, power windows, and wipers.

Suspension & Structural Rust

Push down each car corner to ensure immediate rebound without bouncing; inspect wheel arches, exhaust pipe, and underbody for severe corrosion.

CATEGORY 02

📄 Documentation & Ownership

Verify legal vehicle papers, service records, and loan clearances.

Original RC & Chassis Match

⚠️ Critical Check

Verify original physical Registration Certificate (RC); confirm vehicle chassis number (VIN) and engine number match the RC and physical stamps exactly.

Active Insurance Policy

Confirm the car has active insurance coverage without long lapses; verify No Claim Bonus (NCB) and check policy details match the current owner.

Service History & Odometer Records

Review authorized service records, invoices, or stamped maintenance booklet; ensure chronological mileage logs align with the odometer reading.

Loan Clearance (NOC / Form 35)

If previously bought on loan, ensure bank NOC and signed Form 35 are provided to clear hypothecation before purchase.

Pollution Certificate & Road Tax

Verify current Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate is valid and road tax receipts are cleared without unpaid traffic challans.

CATEGORY 03

🚗 Test Drive & Dynamics

Observe steering, braking, transmission behavior, and warning lights.

Steering Alignment & Handling

On a safe, flat road, test if the car tracks straight without pulling left or right; check for steering wheel vibration or play at 50–70 km/h.

Braking Feel & Stability

Test moderate and firm braking: pedal should feel firm (not spongy), vehicle must stop straight without shuddering or pulling.

Clutch & Gearbox Operation

Manual: clutch pedal bites smoothly without slipping under acceleration. Automatic: shifts up and down cleanly without harsh jerking or hesitation.

Unusual Sounds & Vibrations

Listen for suspension clunks over bumps, metallic rattles, transmission whining, wheel bearing hums, or screeching belts.

Dashboard Warning Lights

Turn on ignition: all warning lights should illuminate momentarily. Start the engine: Check Engine, Airbag, ABS, and Battery lights MUST turn off.
